Tap a Revoke… above — the confirm sheet is live, and the session-key row asks the harder question. The reveal above is deliberately not a sheet: only "I've stored it" removes it.

Why the reveal is inline here

Every other mobile secondary panel is a sheet. A sheet closes on backdrop tap — on a phone, one thumb-slip from losing a shown-once secret. So the reveal lives in the page flow, the key wraps rather than clips, and Copy is a full-width button. Recoverable things (revoke confirm) may be sheets; irrecoverable things may not.
